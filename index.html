About Us

Delivering Quality

"Your Reliable Partner in Food Trading"

At EXPAID TRADING L.L.C, we connect businesses with carefully sourced food products, dependable suppliers, and efficient trading solutions.

  • Quality You Can Count On

    We source a wide range of food products from trusted suppliers, maintaining high standards of quality, freshness, and product consistency.

  • A Diverse Product Range

    From fresh and frozen products to grains, dairy, canned foods, oils, and more, we provide a comprehensive selection to support the needs of businesses across different sectors.

  • Built on Trust & Reliability

    We believe in creating lasting business relationships through dependable supply, competitive sourcing, transparent communication, and responsive customer service.

EXPAID TRADING L.L.C is committed to making food sourcing simple, reliable, and efficient. With trusted supply networks and a customer-focused approach, we deliver solutions that help businesses grow with confidence.

  • Consistent Quality

    We carefully select food products from established suppliers and manufacturers, ensuring every product meets our expectations for quality, freshness, and consistency. Our focus is on providing products businesses can confidently rely on.

  • Efficient Supply Solutions

    With access to dependable sourcing networks, we provide a broad selection of food products to meet diverse business requirements. We focus on efficient procurement, competitive solutions, and a steady supply to support our customers.

  • Partnerships That Matter

    We believe successful business relationships are built on trust, communication, and reliability. Our team works closely with customers to understand their needs and provide responsive service and practical food trading solutions.

Frozen Fish & Seafood Trading

We supply a wide range of frozen fish and seafood products sourced from trusted suppliers. Our focus is on maintaining freshness, quality, and proper cold-chain handling throughout the supply process.

Vegetables & Fruits Trading

We trade a variety of fresh fruits and vegetables selected for quality, freshness, and nutritional value. We ensure reliable sourcing and timely supply to meet the needs of our customers.

Dairy Products Trading

We supply a range of high-quality dairy products suitable for retail, food service, and commercial requirements. Our products are sourced from reliable suppliers with a focus on freshness, hygiene, and consistency.

Dates Trading

We source and supply premium-quality dates in a variety of types and selections. Our focus is on delivering fresh, naturally sweet, and carefully packed dates to meet different customer requirements.

Canned & Preserved Food Trading

We offer a diverse selection of canned and preserved food products designed for convenient and long-lasting use. We work with dependable suppliers to ensure consistent quality, taste, and product safety.

Fresh, Chilled & Frozen Meat Trading

We supply fresh, chilled, and frozen meat products sourced from trusted and reliable suppliers. We prioritize quality, food safety, proper storage, and dependable delivery.

Ghee & Vegetable Oil Trading

We trade a range of ghee and vegetable oils suitable for households, restaurants, and commercial food operations. Our products are carefully sourced to provide quality, purity, and consistent performance.

Grains, Cereals & Legumes Trading

We supply a wide selection of grains, cereals, and legumes for everyday consumption and commercial use. Our sourcing network helps ensure reliable quality, competitive pricing, and consistent availability.

Baby Food Trading

We supply carefully selected baby food products from trusted sources, focusing on quality, safety, and nutritional value. Our range is intended to meet the everyday needs of infants and young children with dependable product standards.
